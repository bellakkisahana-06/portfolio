Observations & Interpretations

Observations Interpretations
The village was populated but not overcrowded. The village has a moderate population and a relatively peaceful living environment.
The main road leading into the village was wider than the internal roads. The main road provides better connectivity. Internal roads may need improvement.
Large trees were present, but were limited along the main road. Limited greenery may contribute to higher temperatures and less shade.
People were busy with daily activities during the morning visit. Residents have active routines and take part in regular work.
Local people were friendly and helped us find Banashankari Temple. The community appears welcoming and willing to help visitors.
Banashankari Temple was quiet, with only a few visitors in morning. The temple may be busier at other times, during festivals or special occasions.
The temple was clean and surrounded by greenery. Regular upkeep and greenery make it a pleasant community space.
Older mud and brick houses stood deeper in the village. They reflect the village's long history and architecture.
Roads became narrower and less developed deeper in the village. Infrastructure is stronger near main areas than inside.
Several agricultural tools were observed. Agriculture supports local livelihoods.
People were involved in small-scale businesses. Small businesses add income to the local economy.
More than one temple was observed in the village. Temples are important to community life.
Some gutters were clogged. Poor drainage may cause stagnant water and sanitation issues.
Scrap materials were observed in some areas. Accumulated scrap suggests a need for better waste management.
Basic facilities included a doctor's facility. Residents have access to essential local services.

Phase 2: Problem Identification

Indirect Questioning & Stakeholder Responses

Indirect Questions Responses Received
1. How do you manage farm work when several activities need to be completed at the same time? We depend on family members and available workers.
2. How do you manage harvesting when workers are busy on other farms? We sometimes have to wait or hire workers from nearby villages.
3. Have you changed your farming methods because of changes in labour availability? Yes, sometimes we try to use machines to reduce manual work.
4. How do you arrange transportation when your crops are ready for sale? We usually contact vehicle owners nearby.
5. How do you decide which market is suitable for selling your crops? We compare prices and transportation expenses.
6. Do you usually get the same workers during every farming season? No, sometimes workers are difficult to find.
7. How do you divide farming responsibilities among family members? Each family member helps with different activities.
8. What do you do when transport is not available on the day of harvesting? We keep the crops safely until transport is arranged.
9. How do you usually learn about new facilities available for farmers? Through other farmers and agricultural officers.
10. What factors do you consider before purchasing pesticides? We consider the price, quality and advice from experts.

Social Challenges Identified

  • 1) Lack of proper storage facilities for agricultural products.
  • 2) Lack of modern farming tools.
  • 3) Financial difficulties faced by farmers.
  • 4) High costs of seeds and fertilizers.
  • 5) Difficulty in getting agricultural loans.
  • 6) Lack of proper storage facilities for agricultural products.

Triangulation

Source Triangulation:

1. Who did you speak to or observe? We interacted with farmers and local residents during our community visit.

2. What are the issues different people reported? Farmers may face problems related to irrigation and farming costs.

3. What was the difference in their views? While some farmers highlighted labor shortages, others emphasized fertilizer and financing expenses.

Finalized Social Challenge

Financial difficulties faced by farmers

Farmers in Amargol may face financial difficulties due to increasing farming expenses, such as seeds, fertilizers, and labor costs. Low crop prices and irregular income can make it difficult for them to manage their household and farming expenses.

Problem Statement

How might we support farmers in Amargol to effectively manage increasing operational expenses, fertilizer costs, and cash-flow fluctuations so they can achieve sustainable financial stability?
